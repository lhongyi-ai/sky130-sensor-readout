# Engineering progress published on October 6, 2026

This checkpoint publishes engineering evidence produced through September 24, 2026. October 6 is the publication and English-edition review date, not a new simulation date. The project remains a **chip-core design and simulation effort**. It is not a fabricated or silicon-measured chip, and the complete core has not passed qualification.

## Immediate next work

1. Resolve selected-model terminal-charge definitions, effective body-network options and the mode-reversal/solver behavior using the retained controlled evidence and an applicable supported explanation or repair.
2. Validate any justified repair in an isolated short-window comparison, then complete the same actual ADC's two-frame and reset-abort baseline/strict comparison. TP−TN, RP, RN and VCM must each satisfy the original full-domain 9.765625 µV difference criterion.
3. After numerical qualification, expand to 12 frames, then full-code linearity, reference-drive, device-noise, PVT and mismatch campaigns.
4. In parallel, resolve CAPM physical/coupling references, internal/external RC reference planes and resistance temperature/version provenance. Complete-array physical integration and formal PEX depend on those qualifications.

The frozen design targets remain unchanged: gains 1/4/16, 12 bits, 100 kS/s, 45 PVT conditions, at least 200 applicable statistical samples, and the original dynamic, power, calibration, linearity and settling limits. They are targets rather than achieved system results.
