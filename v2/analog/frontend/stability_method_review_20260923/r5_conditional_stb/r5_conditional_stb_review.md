# r5 three-gain conditional STB review

Actual exported conditional return ratios were reviewed without changing signs or selecting only a favorable crossing. DM phase margins were 39.07844988040595°, 50.10932406647976° and 66.49772475884187° for gains 1/4/16. G1 and G4 fail the 60° target.

The corresponding unity frequencies were 4.023501 MHz, 1.928528 MHz and 706.5296 kHz. Input-common-mode curves had no observed unity crossing, so PM/GM are not applicable. First-stage CM margins were approximately 80.389/80.621/80.752°; output-CM margins approximately 124.424/124.434/124.386°. All observed crossings and native outputs remain in the adjacent result records.

These are fixed-state single-probe results with other loops closed. Native stable text does not qualify the complete coupled-loop system, PVT, mismatch or switched load. The full stability-method contract remains in `../method_review.md`.
