# CAPM physical data and interconnect-temperature gaps

The reviewed official stack diagram includes CAPM/CAP2M, but does not supply a complete validated thin-electrode/special-dielectric/edge profile and corner definition usable for this extraction. Area capacitance density alone cannot uniquely determine thickness and permittivity.

The official [RCX responsibility document](https://skywater-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/main/rules/rcx.html) gives model/extraction exclusion guidance, but its older M2/via2/three-terminal description does not establish the present M3/via3/two-terminal boundary. Capiche's examined ordinary-metal stack lacks dedicated CAPM/CAP2M conductors and dielectric definitions; Magic height entries are not automatically a qualified Quantus stack.

Public ordinary-metal/via temperature coefficients exist. Their applicable reference, layer binding and actual execution need controls; they cannot be relabeled CAPM electrode parameters. The bounded search did not find a complete supported matching Quantus MIM package, without claiming that no such package exists anywhere.

Required follow-up: credible material/structure or supported technology package, independently referenced multiport coupling controls, geometric RC ownership and a single validated temperature-scaling location. No fitted invented material values or changed specifications were used.
